⚛ Quantum Tunneling

Particle can pass through barrier even if energy is lower.

Quantum Tunneling

In classical physics, a particle cannot pass through a barrier if it does not have enough energy. However, in quantum mechanics, particles behave like waves.

This allows them to "leak" through barriers — a phenomenon known as tunneling.

Potential Barrier

The red wall represents a region of high potential energy. Classically, it blocks the particle.

Probability of Transmission

The chance of passing depends on:

  • Barrier height
  • Barrier width
  • Particle energy

Applications

  • Scanning tunneling microscope
  • Nuclear fusion in stars
  • Semiconductors and quantum devices
